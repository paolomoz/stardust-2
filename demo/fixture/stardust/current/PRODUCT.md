## Users

coffee near me") or word-of-mouth. The site does not segment users
beyond that — it serves the same content to first-time visitors and
return customers.

## Product Purpose

Sell single-origin coffee subscriptions and host the cafe's location
information. The site is a marketing surface for an in-person business
that also ships beans nationally.
