What's in the kitchen this week.

Updated Mondays · 7am Pacific

One Ethiopian Yirgacheffe at the front, two Colombians on rotation, and a Costa Rican honey we've been holding for five months. Each bag ships within 48 hours of roast date — which we print, in plain words, on the side of the bag in Special Elite.

You pick the bag. We pick the brew method. Or vice versa. The roast date is up to us, because the roast date is the only thing on this site that's not up for opinion.

How we got here.

Started in 2018. Two people. One Probat. The decision to not grow into a chain has been the load-bearing constraint of every year since. We sample 100+ lots a season and roast about a dozen of them.

We name the farms. We list the varietal. We tell you the elevation. None of this is a flex — it's the floor.
