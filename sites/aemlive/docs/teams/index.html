Teams

We create dedicated teams in Microsoft Teams for each AEM customer and invite business users, developers, and authors to it to answer your questions about ...

We create dedicated teams in Microsoft Teams for each AEM customer and invite business users, developers, and authors to it to answer your questions about authoring and development, coordinate your launch or migration, and help with best practices.

The Adobe team is globally distributed. During US and European business hours you can expect to receive an answer within a few hours. Outside those times, responses may take a bit longer.

Request to create or join a Microsoft Teams channel via your program in Cloud Manager, or reach out to your Adobe contact. For customers using Slack instead, we can also collaborate with you on Slack.
