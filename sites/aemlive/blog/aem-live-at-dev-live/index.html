aem.live at Adobe Developers Live 2024

For years, Adobe Developers Live has been a live, but virtual event. But real-world events have a special vibe for me and that's why I am excited to announce that, for the first time ever, we’re bringing Adobe Developers Live to you in person at Adobe’s HQ in San Jose, California.

The event will take place on November 12th, and it is a fantastic opportunity to learn about what's new in the AEM world, connect with our product team, and the rest of the community.

If you can’t join in person, this will also be broadcast virtually.

Please use this link to register NOW and tell everyone you know: Register today

Why Attend?

Join us for exclusive insights from Adobe Experience Manager and Commerce teams, alongside industry experts and customers who are redefining customer experiences. You'll gain practical knowledge to enhance your own projects. Stay ahead of the curve by discovering the latest Adobe innovations and learning from real-world examples of best practices. Whether you’re new to Adobe Experience Manager, working through a current project, or a seasoned professional, there’s something here for you. Our sessions are tailored to meet all skill levels, ensuring a valuable experience for everyone.

Sessions with a Focus on Edge Delivery Services

Executive Welcome & Adobe Experience Manager Update!

The session is about exploring the innovation happening within the Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) product team, where exciting developments are just beginning. You'll hear directly from Adobe on their vision for digital experiences, how these advancements are shaping web development, and what to anticipate in the near future.

Sherrie Rohde Developer Relations - Experience Manager - Adobe

Ron Nagy Sr. Evangelist - Adobe

Loni Stark Vice President - Strategy and Product - Adobe &

Cedric Huesler Director Product Management - Experience Manager - Adobe

Edge Delivery Services: One Year In, Six Ways Better!

Lars Trieloff is talking about the first year of Edge Delivery Services in action! He’ll share valuable insights from the engineering team about the challenges they’ve overcome and the key achievements they’ve realized. You’ll discover the lessons learned, discoveries made, and transformations that have shaped the product during its first year, along with an exciting preview of what’s next.

Lars Trieloff Principal - Adobe

Tales from 200 trenches!

Kiran Murugulla and Varun Mitra are diving into tales from 200 trenches, sharing what they’ve learned from over 200 Edge Delivery Services projects. The VIP implementation team reveals what it truly took to deliver fast-performing experiences with excellent Core Web Vitals. Discover the secrets behind successful implementations and what it takes to achieve great performance!

Kiran Murugulla Senior Engineer & Varun Mitra Cloud Architect - Adobe

Universal Editor, AEM Authoring & Edge Delivery Services
What goes together better than Chips & Salsa? Edge Delivery Services & AEM based authoring. Find out how Universal Editor can be used to assemble blocks and provide content authors with the tools to manage content and leverage all the strengths of the AEM they know & love. This session will cover everything you need to know to get your project set up!

Lamont Crook Principal Solutions Consultant - Adobe

Developing exceptional commerce Storefront experiences with Edge Delivery Services & Adobe Commerce!

Surya Lamech and Deck Reyes are talking about what it takes to develop exceptional commerce storefront experiences using Edge Delivery Services and Adobe Commerce. They’ll unpack what makes a great commerce experience, diving into the key components—content, assets, new commerce "drop-ins," extensibility, and integrations. You’ll also get a look at examples of the new frontend-friendly commerce developer experience that leverages Edge Delivery Services.

Surya Lamech Sr. Product Marketing Manager - Enterprise Commerce &
Deck Reyes Practice Lead - AEM Expert Solutions Consulting - Adobe

Forms on the Edge!

Discover how to create seamless enrollment experiences with AEM Forms & Edge Delivery Services, and learn to overcome DIY challenges, scale effectively, integrate forms with data sources, and achieve perfect LightHouse scores while also extending into new channels.

Arun Taneja Principal Product Manager - Experience Manager Forms - Adobe
