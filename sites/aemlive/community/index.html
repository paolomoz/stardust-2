Join the Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Community to learn and build connections with fellow practitioners. We offer diverse engagement options, whether you prefer virtual interactions on Discord, engaging in Adobe Experience League Community, exchanging in AEM User Groups, or attending in-person events.

To accommodate our global community, we host bi-weekly live sessions on Thursday evenings.
Missed a live session? No worries—all recordings are available on Youtube, so you don’t miss anything.

We’re always looking to discuss and explore new ideas and topics in our live events. Please feel free to approach us with any suggestions you would like to see covered. Explore our favorite community hangouts to stay connected to the AEM Community and deepen your engagement with us.

Adobe Champion Program

“The Adobe Champion Program celebrates our most dedicated product experts, helping them advance their careers, expand their influence, and shape the future of Adobe Experience Cloud. It’s a dynamic journey of personal and professional development that positions participants as industry leaders. Each year, applications open for a limited time, and Adobe selects approximately 40 Champions per Experience Cloud product to join a year-long cohort filled with professional growth opportunities.”

Jenna Feldman

Adoption Marketing Manager, AEM
